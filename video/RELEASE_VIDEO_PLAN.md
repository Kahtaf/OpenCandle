# OpenCandle launch film — script-aligned cut

## Core message

OpenCandle replaces shallow chatbot answers with inspectable, finance-specific research in the browser and CLI.

## Format

- 67 seconds
- 1920×1080, 30 fps
- Voiceover-led, with deliberate silent space
- No burned-in captions or marketing paragraphs
- Sparse designed copy in the opening metaphor, followed by the final install command

## Script

> Financial answers shouldn’t be a black box.
>
> General-purpose chatbots give you shallow answers. OpenCandle shows you the research behind them.
>
> OpenCandle is an open-source financial research agent for questions that need more than a quick response.
>
> Ask how fundamentals, price action, options, and the market backdrop fit together. In the browser, OpenCandle builds a plan, chooses finance-specific tools, and works through the evidence before answering.
>
> Inspect quotes, filings, indicators, options, and every source behind them.
>
> OpenCandle understands your question, connects your model to the right financial tools, and gathers the evidence. The answer keeps that trail attached, so you can verify it.
>
> Prefer the command line? Run the same workflow in the OpenCandle CLI, with reasoning steps, tool calls, and evidence visible as it works.
>
> Use your model and data providers. Keep control of the research.
>
> OpenCandle. Open-source financial research you can inspect and verify.
>
> Run it with npx opencandle.

## Accuracy rules

- Build the opening from OpenCandle's own palette, logo, finance-specific evidence language, and editable Remotion browser surface; do not substitute a generic dashboard.
- Use the approved CLI render as the continuation surface; do not invent terminal chrome or fake tool calls.
- Crop personal filesystem paths and model identifiers from the terminal recording.
- Keep the explanatory beat inside the real browser UI; do not reintroduce a technical architecture poster, detached cards, or decorative diagram icons.
- No fabricated price, performance, or provider claims.
- No AI-generated decorative artwork. Product footage and native Remotion graphics are the visuals.
