 - What happens: The option expires worthless. You keep the $198 premium but still own the declining shares.
 - Your return:
     - Unrealized stock loss: -$41.75/share (-$4,175)
     - Realized premium gain: +$1.98/share (+$198)
     - Net Position: Down -$3,977 overall.
 - The Reality Check: The premium provides a tiny cushion, but it does not protect you from significant downside risk. You are still exposed to nearly the full loss of holding the stock.

 ### What Live Broker Quotes Would Change

 If you check this during live trading hours, the actual premium you collect will fluctuate rapidly based on:
 - Implied Volatility (IV): Higher market fear increases option pricing, allowing you to collect larger premiums.
 - Time Decay (Theta): Options lose value as expiration approaches.
 - Underlying Price Moves: As the stock rises closer to the strike, the call option premium increases.
