## Design

The migration follows the established slice pattern:

1. `observe_only`: concept planning metadata is recorded but no concept policy card is injected.
2. `dual_run`: the concept policy card can render while the legacy fallback conceptual-education clause remains present.
3. `replacement_active`: the concept policy card owns no-tool educational answer shape, and only fallback playbook item 14 is omitted for `concept_explainer` turns.

The slice keeps `placeholder_concept_explainer` as the evidence plan because no live data is required for pure conceptual education. The answer contract becomes active and requires the final output to behave as a framework/checklist, avoid concrete trade commitment, and avoid freshness/data-gap requirements.

The existing router and tool-bundle behavior remains authoritative: no-symbol conceptual education keeps no active finance tools. This change does not move shared base-role reminders or output-format reminders; those remain until broader shared prompt cleanup has its own parity gate.
